About Us

Who We Are

Christians in Seattle at UW is a student community where students can connect, read the Bible, build friendships, and explore faith together.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to help UW students come to a mature, full, and personal knowledge of God through Bible reading, meetings, fellowship, singing, and other student activities.

What We Do

  • Bible studies / 圣经阅读
  • Weekly fellowship gatherings / 每周交通
  • Friday family gatherings / 周五家庭聚会
  • Singing and spiritual fellowship / 诗歌与属灵交通
  • Saturday hiking / 周六爬山
  • Community events / 社区活动

Student-Led RSO

This is a UW Registered Student Organization. Activities and leadership are organized by UW students.

Meeting rooms and event details may change. Please check Instagram, Discord, or the latest event listing before attending.
